Please read this first

This page runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are stored on this device only and are never sent to any server except OpenAI, which receives one question and one answer at a time so it can understand what you said.

Because there is no server, your OpenAI key is held in this browser tab. Use this on a device you trust and do not use it on a shared or public computer. This tool asks for your Social Security number and bank account numbers. You can say skip at any of those questions and fill them in by hand later.

This is a preparation worksheet. It is not an application, and it does not send anything to the Social Security Administration.
