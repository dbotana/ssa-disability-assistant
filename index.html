Before we start

Please read this first

Everything happens on this computer. When you speak, your words are turned into text here, by a speech model stored with this program — the recording is never sent anywhere. Your answers are saved in this browser only, and nothing is sent to any company or server.

If you choose a PIN below, your answers are saved in this browser, encrypted with it, so you can stop and come back. Your Social Security number and bank numbers are never saved: if you stop partway, you will be asked for them again. Use this on a device you trust and do not use it on a shared or public computer. You can say skip at any sensitive question and fill it in by hand later.

The first question asks which form you are filling out. This tool fills in the forms for you to review, sign, and send yourself. It does not send anything to the Social Security Administration or to Maine's Department of Health and Human Services.

How do you want to answer?

This only sets how the interview starts. Speaking and typing are both available on every question, and you can move between them whenever you like.

With this on I repeat what I heard after every answer, and you press the space bar (or say yes) to keep it. Turn it off to move faster if you can read the screen — your Social Security number, bank numbers and dates are always read back either way, because a wrong digit there is costly.

At least 4 characters; longer is safer. Your answers are saved on this computer, encrypted with this PIN, so you can finish later — you will need it to continue. Leave it blank to save nothing: your answers will be lost when you close this page. Saved answers are deleted after 7 days.

Off, they show only their last four digits, so they cannot be read over your shoulder or in a screen recording. They are always read aloud in full, so you can check every digit.

If you saved your answers as a file on another device, choose it here to pick up where you left off. This replaces any answers saved on this device.

Hold the space bar (or the button) to speak, or press T to type. Press Escape to leave the text box and use the space bar again. When I read an answer back, tap Space to keep it or press N to answer again — you only have to speak if I got it wrong.

Things you can say at any time
  • Space or Yes — after I read back what I heard, to keep it
  • N or No — answer again, without hearing the question repeated
  • Repeat that — hear the question again
  • Go back — return to the previous question
  • Skip this — leave it blank and move on
  • Where am I — hear your progress
  • Read back my answers — hear what you have given so far
  • Save and quit — stop and keep your place
  • Add another condition — from the review screen, to put a new entry on a list
  • Start over — erase everything and begin again
