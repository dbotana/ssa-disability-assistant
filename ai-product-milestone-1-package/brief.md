# AI Product Brief — Milestone 1

## Product value case

Address who cares and why, what you propose to make, how it could create value,
and how you tentatively plan to do it. Focus on the one to three most important
points for each question.

## Engineering credibility checks

Address what must be true, how you will test it, the one to three most important
risks, and where AI is materially necessary. Keep the scope feasible within one
semester.
